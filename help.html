NEDIKE is a student-focused skills and services platform designed to help students showcase their abilities, discover services, explore tasks and develop useful skills.
NEDIKE is designed primarily for students who want to develop their skills, offer services, discover opportunities and connect with other members of the student community.

Services are skills or professional tasks that users can showcase to other members. Examples include graphic design, web development, photography, writing and other professional services.
Visit the Services section and use the available service creation option to provide details about your offering, including its title, description, category and price. The actual publishing options depend on the current platform implementation.
Visit the Services page to browse available listings. Use the search and category options where available to narrow down your choices. Explore services.
Service pricing should be reviewed carefully before publishing. Editing an existing listing depends on the service-management features available in the current version.
Tasks are opportunities where users can request or offer help with specific work. They are intended to encourage collaboration and give students opportunities to apply their skills.
Visit the Tasks section to explore the available opportunities. Use the search and filtering options if they are available. Explore tasks.
The Tasks section is designed to support task creation and discovery. Follow the available instructions on the page to create a task where that functionality is enabled.
