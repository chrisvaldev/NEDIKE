OUR STORY

More than a platform. A place for student potential.

Across university communities, students possess valuable abilities beyond what they learn in lecture halls. Some design graphics, develop websites, take photographs, write content, teach others or provide useful services.

However, finding the right people to offer these services, discovering available opportunities and connecting skills with real needs can be challenging.

NEDIKE was conceived to help bridge this gap. It provides a dedicated digital space where students can present their abilities, discover services, connect around tasks and explore opportunities to learn and grow.

We believe that a student's potential should not be limited to the classroom. Every useful skill has the possibility to create value.

OUR PURPOSE

What drives NEDIKE forward.

Our purpose is centred on making student skills more visible, accessible and connected to opportunities.

01 / OUR MISSION

Empowering student talent.

To provide a platform that connects students' skills and services with people who need them, while encouraging collaboration, practical experience, learning and personal growth.

02 / OUR VISION

A community of opportunity.

To build an accessible student-focused ecosystem where talent is recognised, skills are developed, opportunities are discovered and students can create value through what they know.
