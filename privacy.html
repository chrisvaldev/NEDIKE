Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains how NEDIKE handles information when you use our platform and interact with the student community.

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the intended privacy practices for NEDIKE, a student-focused skills, services and opportunities platform. Please read it carefully before creating an account or submitting personal information.

1. Introduction

NEDIKE is designed to help students showcase their abilities, discover services, connect with other students and explore opportunities for personal and professional development.

This policy explains what information may be collected, how it may be used, and the choices available to users regarding their personal information.

By using NEDIKE, you acknowledge that you have read this policy. Where applicable law requires consent, we will seek that consent separately.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, NEDIKE may handle the following categories of information:

  • Account information: Name, email address and account details supplied during registration.
  • Academic information: Institution, course of study and current academic level.
  • Profile information: Profile picture, biography, location, skills and interests you choose to provide.
  • Marketplace information: Service descriptions, task listings, budgets, applications and related activity you submit.
  • Communication information: Messages and other information you choose to share through platform features, where implemented.
  • Technical information: Limited device or browser information may be processed by hosting providers or platform infrastructure, depending on the deployed environment.

We aim to collect only information that is relevant to providing and improving the platform.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be used for purposes such as:

  • Creating and maintaining user profiles.
  • Supporting account-related features.
  • Displaying services, skills and task listings.
  • Helping users discover relevant opportunities.
  • Supporting communication and interaction between users where available.
  • Improving the usability, reliability and functionality of NEDIKE.
  • Responding to user enquiries and addressing platform issues.
  • Protecting the platform and its users against misuse, where appropriate.

4. Information Visibility

Some information submitted to NEDIKE may be visible to other users, depending on the feature and how it is implemented.

  • Profile details may be displayed to other platform users.
  • Published service listings may be visible to people browsing the marketplace.
  • Task descriptions and related details may be visible to users exploring available tasks.
  • Information shared in communications may be accessible to the intended participants.

Avoid publishing passwords, financial account details, private identification numbers or other sensitive information in public profile fields, service descriptions or task listings.

5. Data Storage and Security

The current NEDIKE prototype uses browser-based localStorage for certain account, profile, listing and activity information. This means that some information may remain on the device and browser where it was entered.

Browser localStorage is not the same as a secure, centrally managed database. Information stored this way may be accessible to someone who can access the same browser profile or device.

NEDIKE is being developed progressively. A production backend, centralized data management, and additional security controls should be implemented and reviewed before the platform is used to process real users' sensitive information at scale.

Security reminder: Do not reuse passwords carelessly, share your login details, or enter confidential information into public profile or listing fields.

6. Information Sharing

NEDIKE does not intend to sell users' personal information. Information may be visible to other users when necessary for features such as profiles, service discovery and task listings.

If third-party hosting, analytics, communication or other service providers are introduced, relevant information may be processed by those providers to support the platform, subject to appropriate safeguards and applicable legal requirements.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or where necessary to address legitimate security, safety or legal concerns.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

The current prototype uses browser localStorage to maintain certain information between visits. This may include account state, profile information, listings, onboarding details and notifications.

LocalStorage is stored in your browser and is not automatically synchronized across different devices or browsers.

Clearing browser site data may remove information stored by the prototype. This may affect your locally saved account state and other platform activity.

If cookies or additional tracking technologies are introduced in future versions, this policy should be updated to explain their use.

8. Data Retention

Information stored in the current prototype may remain in the browser until it is changed or deleted by the user or removed through browser data management.

Future versions using centralized storage should define appropriate retention periods based on the purpose of processing, legal obligations and user requests.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, users may have rights to:

  • Request access to personal information held about them.
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where applicable.
  • Object to or request restriction of certain processing activities where legally available.
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.
  • Raise concerns with the relevant data protection authority.

For the current browser-based prototype, users may be able to edit or clear some locally stored information through the platform or their browser settings. This does not necessarily remove information held by other users, screenshots, exports or external services.

In Nigeria, personal data processing is subject to applicable data protection laws, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

10. Age and Eligibility

NEDIKE is intended for university and other eligible tertiary-level students and members of its intended community.

Users should provide accurate information and should not create accounts on behalf of another person without proper authority.

If you believe a minor's personal information has been submitted improperly, please contact the platform administrator so the matter can be reviewed.

11. External Links and Third-Party Services

NEDIKE may contain links to external websites or services. These websites operate independently and may have their own privacy policies and practices.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of external services before sharing personal information with them.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated as NEDIKE develops new features, changes its technology or responds to applicable legal requirements.

The latest revision date will be displayed at the beginning of this page. Users should review this page periodically for updates.

13. Contact and Privacy Enquiries

If you have a question, concern or request relating to this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please use the available NEDIKE support channel.

Visit the Help Centre for available support information.

NEDIKE should publish a verified privacy contact address and identify the responsible data controller before public production deployment.
